## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- Deterministic study planner: allocation by days left, confidence and stakes, taper before exams, breaks, meals, commitments, carry-over of missed work, deadlines.
- Screens: Today, Exams, Week, Stats, Settings, setup wizard, public landing page at `/about`.
- Daily email (HTML and text) via Resend, SMTP or a local outbox, with missed-send catch-up.
- Installable PWA with offline-tolerant task toggles.
- CSV and calendar (.ics) import, demo seed data, Docker and docker-compose.
